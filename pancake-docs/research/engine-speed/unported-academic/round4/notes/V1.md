## Q1. Does each number the plan or notes cite appear in the paper?

### Takeaway
Four of the six papers hold the number the plan cites, but three of those four need the context fixed (Athena, HOBBIT, ProMoE). Cache-Conditional holds its miss-rate and speedup claims, but the plan's "2.9% tokens differ" is not the paper's 2.9% (that is Mixtral perplexity). DISCO's 10.3% and 31.4% are real but are four-dataset averages, and the plan's "7.9% ceiling" is not in the paper. The ProMoE abstract's "2.07x average" decode figure is not a body average.

### Inferences
- A44 is a real gain in the paper, but it is Athena (a SARSA learner with four state features, an epsilon-greedy policy, and an uncorrelated-reward term) against MAB (DUCB, no state). The paper's own ablation says "the stateless Athena ... performs slightly worse than MAB" (ablation text p13, Fig. 18), and the increments that bring it to the full Athena are +1.4, +1.7, +0.8, +0.1 (state features) and +1.0 (reward correction). So a plain stateless epoch bandit on our opt-in arms is not what the paper measured. The "+5%" is the value of the state and the reward, on CPU-simulated prefetch and off-chip decisions, not of a bandit over window types. Treat A44's "+5%" as a bound on a different mechanism.
- DISCO's 10.3% and 31.4% are latency improvements averaged over four datasets against tuned static and heuristic baselines, on A100 80GB with greedy decoding (temperature 0). They are not per-workload gains and they do not show the chain-stop rule alone (the classifier also uses the position and draft top-k). A per-workload or edit-only number for our engine is still missing.
- AdaEDL's abstract "10%-57%" is over static draft lengths, not over an entropy-free stop; against the top-1 stop it is 0.2% to 9.3% (mean about 3.2%, derived). So the plan's "0-3% in practice" is consistent with the paper's table when read as a mean; the upper end is for CNN-DM at long draft lengths.
- HOBBIT's accuracy claim and its speedups are for edge boards and two 8- or 16-expert models, with baselines not used in the Pancake engine. The paper's own ablation (1.19x to 1.57x) is the number that transfers to dynamic loading; the 9.93x is not a Pancake estimate.
- Cache-Conditional's 2x on-device gain is for Qwen1.5-MoE-A2.7B on CPU llama.cpp with flash-backed experts, normalized to LRU at equal cache size. Our GPU/CPU split is different (the paper's loss is flash reads per token), so the 2x is a bound on the mechanism, not a prediction for the Pancake engine.
- ProMoE's 1.09x (llama.cpp decode) is the smallest of its decode figures and comes against a per-layer reactive LRU baseline; the paper itself says LRU thrashing drives most of its prefill gain. The plan's "est. 0-3%" is closer to the paper's decode number than the abstract's 2.07x.

### Gaps
- Athena: the number is verified; the full text was read, so the round 2 "partial" note is superseded. No per-workload CPU result for our bandit arms exists in the paper.
- DISCO: the "7.9%" in the plan is not in the paper; find its source or drop it. Per-dataset values in Table 5 are garbled in the text layer, so only the averages are quoted.
- AdaEDL: no number for entropy versus top-1 at our draft length (3 to 7 tokens) on a GPU at temperature 0 (the paper uses temperature 0.7 and 1.0 and FP32 on A100).
- HOBBIT: Fig. 5(b) threshold profile and Fig. 16 per-config values are figures only; the 67/30/3 split is the text value.
- Cache-Conditional: Figure 9 values were read from the text layer of the figure; they agree with the notes but a visual check of the bars was not done.
- ProMoE: the 2.07x abstract number cannot be reproduced from the body; Figs. 10 and 11 values were read from text, not from the bars.

## Q2. Setup of each claim, and exact or lossy

### Takeaway
Only DISCO and ProMoE-style prefetch are exact in output. Cache-Conditional and HOBBIT are lossy (they change which expert weights are used). AdaEDL is exact (its stop rule only changes draft length; verification is rejection sampling). Athena is a performance policy, so output is not changed by its own decisions, but the effect is through lossy arms in our engine.

### Cited Findings
- **Athena 2601.17615**: setup: ChampSim trace-driven simulator; 100 memory-intensive single-core traces (SPEC CPU 2006 (29), SPEC 2017 (20), PARSEC (13), Ligra (13), CVP (25)) at >= 3 LLC MPKI, plus 90 four-core and 90 eight-core mixes; bandwidth-constrained; baseline MAB (DUCB) and HPAC and TLP; Athena has 3 KB storage per core. Exactness: not applicable (a prefetch/off-chip policy; no change to results) [Athena PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.17615), §6.1, Table 6, p8-9 (ChampSim on p8; the 90 + 90 multi-core mixes on p9).
- **DISCO 2405.04304**: setup: Starcoder-15B target with 168M draft (code: MBPP, HumanEval); Vicuna-13B target (CNN-DM, Alpaca); single A100 80GB; greedy decoding (temperature 0) for Table 1; temperature 1 only for CNN-DM Table 6 (p12). Exactness: exact in the greedy setting, "while preserving the same output as the target model" (p4). [DISCO PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.04304)
- **AdaEDL 2410.18351**: setup: Llama2-7B target, Llama2-Drafter-115M draft, sampling temperature 0.7 (Table 1), single A100 80GB, FP32; datasets Dolly-15k, WMT-19, CNN-DM; metric is average token rate in TPS (p5). Exactness: exact (rejection sampling "will preserve the distribution of the original target model", p2). [AdaEDL PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.18351)
- **Cache-Conditional 2412.00099** (titled "Mixture of Cache-Conditional Experts" in the PDF): setup: Qwen1.5-MoE-A2.7B, DeepSeek-V2-Lite, Phi-3.5-MoE, Mixtral-8x7B; Wikitext, MMLU, GSM8K; on-device Qwen1.5-MoE on two Snapdragon Android phones (12 GB and 16 GB RAM), llama.cpp on CPU, mlock; 4-bit experts (p12-13). Exactness: lossy (Wikitext perplexity +0.1% to +3%; accuracy under 0.1% on MMLU and GSM8K, abstract p1). [Cache-Conditional PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.00099)
- **HOBBIT 2411.01433**: setup: Mixtral-8x7B and Phi-MoE; RTX 4090 (24 GB, 256 GB host RAM, PCIe 4.0) and Jetson AGX Orin (32 GB unified memory); baselines Transformers, DeepSpeed-Inference, Llama.cpp, MoE-Offloading, MoE-Infinity, Fiddler; implemented on Llama.cpp (p9-11). Exactness: lossy (mixed precision; accuracy within 1%, p11). [HOBBIT PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.01433)
- **ProMoE 2410.22134**: setup: RTX 4090 (24 GB), Intel i9-14900K, 128 GB host RAM, PCIe 4.0 (32 GB/s stated, 23.9 GB/s measured) (p1, p8); models DeepSeek-MoE, Qwen (FP16), Mixtral (INT4); ShareGPT, batch 1; transformers and llama.cpp codebases; baselines static, LRU, UM, TO/LO (p8-9). Exactness: exact in output (it changes only prefetch and copy order; the paper does not report any output change). [ProMoE PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.22134)

### Inferences
- For our engine, only the exact items (A7 chunked promotions, A22 stop rule under greedy, A21 exact sampled speculation) can be taken at face value for output; A1, A4 and A27 change routing or precision and must be gated by quality tests.

### Gaps
- Exactness for DISCO outside greedy decoding is not claimed by the paper beyond "Since DISCO is sampling temperature agnostic" (p12); no output-equality test was run at temperature > 0.
