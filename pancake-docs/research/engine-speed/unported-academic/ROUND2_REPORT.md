# Logs and one trace settle most academic items

Eleven items reduce to eight measurements, one logging change, and two waits, and most of the cheap decisions need no GPU. Start with two log-only analyses: A40 (tool time that decode could overlap) and A41 (edits without a read, or "done" without a test). Each takes half a day to a day, and either can remove its item. One night trace run then records per-position top-1 probability, draft entropy, and target and drafter probabilities for the drafted token. That single run sizes the A22 entropy rule and provides the A56 metric. A49's five-point context profile (about 1 GPU hour) tells A34 and A32 where to sweep and stream. A44 is the cheapest GPU test (20 to 30 minutes on existing `STRATA_BANDIT` arms). No item has a measured gain on Pancake yet; A19 is capped near 5% of agent turn time and is the most likely to come out small. A27 and A28 wait on a routing trace with gate weights, and on free disk plus owner approval for a 58 GB download. Every "full text" read below is a summarizing fetch, not verbatim text, so no figure should be quoted before a PDF check.

## 3. Recommended order

1. **Log-only day (A40, A41, plus checks).** Measure the A40 overlap and the A41 counts from existing logs, and check whether turn logs keep per-token logprobs (A19) and whether the `STRATA_BANDIT` decision log exists (A44). Reason: zero GPU, can remove two items, and fixes the test cost of A19 and A44.
2. **One night trace run (A22, A56).** Record per draft position: top-1 probability, full entropy, p(d), q(d), and argmax match. Reason: one run decides whether the entropy rule beats top-1 and yields the A56 metric.
3. **Context profile (A49).** Check the KV size formula, then profile decode at 2k, 16k, 32k, 64k and 128k with deferral on. Reason: the cheapest informative GPU run, and it sets the context where A34 and A32 matter.
4. **Bandit A/B (A44).** Fixed-off, fixed-on, and bandit with margin 0.02, A B B A, on edit and chat windows. Reason: existing arms and a clear pass criterion, about 30 minutes of GPU.
5. **Thinking replay (A19).** CPU replay if logprobs exist, otherwise one night run. Reason: run only if the thinking share of logged turns is above about 10%, since the ceiling is 5.5%.
6. **Routing dump, then coverage (A27 gate, A32).** One `--dump-routing` run gives gate weights for A27 and distinct-expert counts for A32. Read the PCIe link state, and time per-layer expert compute only if the coverage knee falls in a usable ubatch range. Reason: one dump serves two items.
7. **One sweep pass (A34).** Bit-exact logit check, then one coordinate-descent pass on the decode GEMV. Reason: last among the measurements, because no RDNA3 evidence exists and the context points come from step 3.
8. **Wait (A27, A28).** A27 needs step 6 gate weights and a sub-2-bit source. A28 needs owner approval for a 58 GB download, free disk (D has 26 GB free), and a check that the engine loads the GSQ and LUT formats. Reason: neither can be tested on this box today.

## 4. Open risks and unverified items

- [READ LEVEL] Every full-text read in these notes is a WebFetch summary, not verbatim text. Abstract-only items: Ansor 2406.20037, CLTune, SVIP 2411.18462, GSQ 2604.18556. Snippet-only items: Kernel Tuner HIP, MoE-Infinity, OSDI headline numbers, Copilot overlap.
- [UNVERIFIED] IQ2_XS at about 2.3 bpw and IQ1_M at about 1.75 bpw are from memory. The 25% byte saving in A27 depends on them.
- [UNVERIFIED] Copilot trace overlap of 7.7% (secondary write-up). TraceLab's per-request figures sum to 100.8%, which the paper does not explain.
- [UNVERIFIED] MoE-Infinity 50 to 160 ms (snippet). OSDI 1,200 and 1,800 tokens/s (snippet). Kernel Tuner 10x (snippet).
- [UNVERIFIED] ISTA-DASLab numbers are vendor-reported with no independent evaluation. The Qwen card's 8 GB VRAM claim has no test. The Qwen card lists 176.9B parameters, while its HF metadata says 117B.
- [OPEN] Whether Strata's MTP path returns more than the top-1 probability. Round 1 says only top-1 is returned, which sets the cost of the A22 entropy rule.
- [OPEN] Whether turn logs keep per-token logprobs. The A19 replay depends on it.
- [OPEN] Whether the engine can load GSQ or 3.5-bit LUT expert formats (A28). Round 1 calls the LUT kernel a project.
- [OPEN] KV bytes per token for the product models (GQA or MLA). If the 128k KV with experts does not fit 20 GB, the 128k point measures offload, not attention.
- [OPEN] Routing skew. The A32 coverage numbers assume uniform routing, so they are an upper bound on distinct experts per ubatch.
- [RISK] The thinking-stop evidence comes from Qwen3-4B-Thinking on BFCL; Strata's families are untested. The 22% thinking share rests on 34 logged turns.
- [RISK] Disk: D has 26 GB free (97% used) and the root disk has about 16 GB. A 58 GB or 111 GB download is blocked. Runs for A34 and A49 need a model on D.
- [RISK] No paper tests these methods on RDNA3 (gfx1100) or ROCm. Athena and SliceMoE are simulations (ChampSim, mobile SoC). Every GPU item runs on the night queue under the A B B A and discard-first-run rules.
