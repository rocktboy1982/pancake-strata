## What else the bodies change

- ProMoE abstract vs body: the abstract's "2.20x prefill and 2.07x decode" averages the llama.cpp and transformers numbers
  against the offloading baselines (UM, LO, TO). Against LRU in llama.cpp decode it is 1.09x. Do not use the abstract
  figure for A7.
- ProMoE batch 1 numbers (section 7.5, QW-2, llama.cpp): decode 1.16x over LRU at batch 1, 1.34x at batch 4. Cache-rate
  sweep (7.4, QW-2): 1.14x average over LRU.
- HOBBIT: the dynamic scorer works only on a cache miss. That matches the plan's "skip only on misses" use and is the one
  part that transfers structurally; the precision swap does not (no copy below IQ2_XS), as the plan says.
- DALI cost model and the plan's own argument agree: per-expert CPU cost nearly flat in tokens makes the greedy rule inert
  at one-user decode. This is a reading of the cost model, not a DALI result.
- Athena and Marconi share the lesson for the plan's wording: both papers' headline gains are over their own simple
  baselines inside a simulator or a multi-user server. None is a prediction for this engine.
